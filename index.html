Chef's Special Note & Terms

Welcome to Urban Eats! Please read our chef's note carefully. All our ingredients are sourced fresh daily from local farmers. We take pride in our zero-waste policy and sustainable cooking practices. If you have any allergies, please inform our staff before ordering. Our kitchen is nut-free, but we do process dairy and gluten. Reservations are recommended for groups of 6 or more. Cancellations must be made 24 hours in advance. We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone. Thank you for dining with us and supporting local agriculture. We hope you enjoy your meal and have a wonderful experience at Urban Eats. Don't forget to check out our daily specials board near the entrance!
